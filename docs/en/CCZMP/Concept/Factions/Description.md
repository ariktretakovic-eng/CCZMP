## ZONESTROY
ZONASTROY (State Construction Corporation NChG)

 Base location:
Industrial zone "Oktyabrskaya", eastern outskirts of Pripyat.

 Brief description of the faction:
ZONASTROY is the main construction organization of the Independent Chernobyl State. After the disaster, it was their engineers and workers who began to rebuild the destroyed areas, strengthen the perimeters and build shelters for new bases. Their motto is “If it collapses, we will raise it!”
They are not afraid to work under radiation and psi-storms, because without them the zone would have long ago turned into a pile of scrap metal.

 Faction transport:
— KAMAZ concrete mixer
— Excavator EO-3323
— Crawler crane KS-4561
— Pickup truck for engineering personnel
— Homemade armored car “Miner” (in later ranks)

 Initial ranks (junior staff):

worker,

Mason,

Civil Engineer,

Site master,

Foreman,

 Faction tasks:

Construction and restoration of buildings NChG

Creation of new fortifications, roadblocks and bridges

Construction of protective domes and underground shelters

Cooperation with ChernEnergo and ComNet in the construction of energy and communication networks

Repair of strategically important facilities after emissions

## Court NChG
Court of the Independent Chernobyl State (Court NChG)

 Base location:

The Court building NChG is located in the central part of Pripyat, not far from Independence Square.
It is a massive concrete structure partially rebuilt from the ruins of an old government building.
In the courtyard there is parking for official vehicles and an isolated entrance for the detainee convoy.
The territory also houses an archive of court cases and a meeting room equipped with communication and video recording systems.

 Brief description of the faction:

Court NChG is the highest judicial authority in the Zone.
He is independent from the Investigative Committee and the Prosecutor's Office, but interacts with them within the framework of the law.
The purpose of the Court is to ensure fair and impartial hearing of cases: from minor administrative violations to serious crimes against NChG.

Judges have broad powers, but are also fully responsible to the Presidential Council.
In the Zone, the Court is the final authority where the fate of anyone is decided: a stalker, an officer, an official or the president of a clan.

 Faction transport:

Audi A6 (official vehicle of the judge) - used for travel and escort.

Volkswagen Passat (for delivery of cases and documents) - transport for judicial office work.

UAZ “Bukhanka” (convoy transport) - used to deliver the accused.

Mercedes-Benz S-Class (Chairman of the Court) is the official car of the senior team.

 Initial ranks (junior staff):

Court secretary - responsible for processing cases, documentation and receiving applications.,

Assistant judge - keeps minutes, prepares materials for meetings.,

Bailiff - controls the execution of sentences and court decisions.,

Magistrate - considers minor and administrative cases.,

Judge of first instance - conducts full trials in criminal cases.

 Faction tasks:

Conducting court hearings on cases transferred from IC and the Prosecutor's Office.

Making judgments and decisions based on evidence.

Ensuring fair and impartial proceedings.

Control over the execution of court decisions through bailiffs.

Maintaining a register of cases and verdicts, accessible only to government agencies.

Possibility of reviewing cases on appeals and complaints.

Protecting the rights of citizens NChG and maintaining the rule of law throughout the Zone.


## Investigative Committee NChG
Investigative Committee NChG (IC NChG)

 Base location:

The main department IC NChG is located in the central sector of Pripyat, the building of the former administrative complex No. 14 (rumored to be the former KGB headquarters).
A fence with a surveillance system and a checkpoint has been installed around the territory.
On the territory of the base there is a garage, a temporary detention center, an interrogation room and a case archive.

 Brief description of the faction:

The Investigative Committee NChG is an elite security agency reporting directly to the President of the Independent Chernobyl State.
IC NChG is investigating crimes in the zone, corruption within government agencies, as well as collecting information about illegal organizations and trade in artifacts.

The investigators of this structure are not just “officials with folders.”
They are armed, trained and have the authority to act on par with special forces in the event of a threat to national security.
Many stalkers and even military personnel try to avoid IC - no one wants to “get into their report.”

 Faction transport:

Toyota Land Cruiser 200 (armored car IC) - used for traveling to crime scenes or escorting detainees.

UAZ Patriot (operational group) - the main vehicle for patrolling.

Mercedes-Benz E-Class (investigator's official car) - for senior ranks.

Mi-8 helicopter IC (rarely, only by order of the head) - for traveling on particularly dangerous operations or evacuation.

 Initial ranks (junior staff):

Trainee investigator - undergoes training and assists senior officers.,

Junior investigator - receives the first cases, conducts interrogations under the supervision of a mentor.,

Investigator - conducts independent investigations, participates in raids.,

Senior Investigator - manages a group of junior employees, gains access to confidential cases.,

The investigator for especially important cases is a junior elite who has the right to detain officials.

Faction tasks:

Conducting interrogations, searches and detentions of suspects in cases NChG.

Conducting criminal cases against lawbreakers and corrupt officials.

Monitoring the actions of FSIN, traffic police, military and customs for abuse of authority.

Gathering intelligence about criminal gangs and artifact dealers.

Conducting special operations jointly with other structures (order of the President).

Reporting to the Security Council NChG and the President personally.

## Service station "Kaban" (Kabanovtsy)
Group: Service station "Kaban"

 General description:

STO "Kaban" is a neutral (well, almost, more official) group of technicians and repairmen, living on the border of the Landfill and the Equipment Graveyard. They are known in the zone as experts in repairing, restoring and upgrading equipment and weapons.
For the right price, they can build you an armored car from junk or install tuning on your AKM so that it will shoot like a sniper.

They do not participate in faction wars, preferring to remain on the sidelines and make money from both bandits and the military. However, if someone touches them, the response will be tough and organized.
