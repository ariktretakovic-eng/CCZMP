## Rules
Reasons for blocking a bank account/player card (NBZ) - People's Bank of the Zone

 1. Money laundering

Using false transactions or schemes to “twist” funds between accounts.

Blocking: forever + SBU investigation + withdrawal of funds

 2. Exploiting project vulnerabilities

An attempt at dupe/bugs/buggy translations.

Blocking: up to 30 days, possibly permanently if the damage is large.

 3. Transfers from fake accounts

Receiving money from suspicious or inactive accounts (including multi-accounts).

Blocking: 7-14 days, account freezing until clarification.

 4. Interference with the work of the NBZ

Attempt to hack/interfere with the bank's server, hacker attacks.

Blocking: forever, a hunt by the SBU may also be announced.

 5. Withdrawing funds from someone else's account

PIN code theft, PDA hacking, access to other people's accounts.

Blocking: forever, with possible imprisonment.

 6. Payment for illegal services through a bank

Ordering murders, buying illegal weapons, bribing officials, etc.

Blocking: 30 days, the account goes into monitoring mode.

 7. Credit fraud

Receiving a loan without the purpose of repayment, or transferring funds immediately after registration (in order to hide the trace).

Blocking: 14-30 days, accrual of debts, seizure of property.

 8. Mass transfer of funds (black transfers)

Transfers to a large number of small accounts (laundering).

Blocking: up to 7 days + verification by the security department.

 9. Storage of illegal funds

Money received from the sale of artifacts (if you are a state), orders in the Black Market, etc.

Blocking: up to 14 days, confiscation of funds.

 10. Incorrect documentation

Mismatch between passport and account holder data.

Blocking: until the error is resolved.
