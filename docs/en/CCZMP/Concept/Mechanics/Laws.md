## Criminal Code
Criminal Code - Criminal Code, all prohibitions and what will happen for violating them:
Article 1. Infliction of bodily injury/murder

1.1. Causing bodily injury to a citizen. The violator is imprisoned for 3 years.

1.2. Causing bodily injury to a government employee. structures, the violator is imprisoned for 5 years.

1.3. Murder of a citizen with a firearm. The offender is imprisoned for a period of 5 years with the confiscation of his weapons license.

1.4. Murder of a government employee. structures with firearms. The offender is imprisoned for a period of 5 years with the confiscation of his weapons license.

1.5. Murder by negligence (with fists). The offender is imprisoned for 3 years.

1.6. Attack on high-ranking officials. The offender is punished by imprisonment for 5 years.

Article 2. Hooliganism

2.1. Hooliganism. The violator is punished by imprisonment for a period of 2 years.

2.2. Damage to the state transport. The violator is punished by imprisonment for a period of 2 years.

2.3. Insulting government employees The offender is imprisoned for 3 years

2.4. Insulting high-ranking officials, the offender is imprisoned for 5 years

2.5. For false information, namely impersonation of an employee of government agencies, a person is punished by imprisonment for a period of 2 years

2.6. For threats of violence/physical harm, the offender is imprisoned for 1 year

2.7. For threats of violence/physical harm to high-ranking officials and government employees. structures, the violator is imprisoned for 4 years

Article 3. Terrorism

3.1. Terrorist act. (Shooting people on the street, taking hostages). The offender is imprisoned for a period of 5 years. If it is a group of persons, then imprisonment for a period of 5 years.

3.2. Sale of weapons. The offender is punished with imprisonment

for 3 years.

3.3. Carrying an open weapon is regarded as terrorism, and the violator receives 5 years in prison. 

Article 4. Bribes

4.1. Offering a bribe to an official. The violator is imprisoned for 3 years.

4.2. Taking a bribe by an official. The offender must be fired and imprisoned for 5 years

4.3. Release from custody for a bribe, the violator must be dismissed from the authorities, included in the emergency organization, imprisoned for a period of 5 years

Article 5. Penetration

5.1. Penetration into army territory. The violator is imprisoned for 4 years.

5.2. Staying in the FSB office unaccompanied by an FSB officer. The violator is deprived of freedom for 3 years.

5.3. Staying on FSB territory unaccompanied by an FSB officer. The violator is deprived of freedom for 3 years.

5.4. Staying in a closed service parking lot adjacent to the building MVD, unaccompanied by a MVD/FSB officer. The violator is imprisoned for 3 years.

Article 6. Disobedience

6.1. For non-compliance, the violator is imprisoned for 1 year.

6.2. For repeated non-compliance, the offender is imprisoned for 3 years.

Article 7. Sale/use **

7.1. Sale | use ** the offender is imprisoned for 5 years.

7.2. Possession of **, the offender is punishable by imprisonment for a period of 5 years

7.3. Storage of stolen materials. The violator is imprisoned for 2 years.

Article 8. Vehicle theft

8.1. Vehicle theft (if proven): up to 2 years in prison.

8.2. Theft or attempted theft of military equipment imprisonment for a period of 5 years

Article 9. Complicity

9.1. Complicity in a crime: From 1 year to 5 years in prison. It all depends on the crime being charged.

Article 10. Road accident

10.1. Leaving the scene of an accident. The offender is imprisoned for 3 years.

10.2. Creating a situation for an accident. The offender is imprisoned for 2 years.

Article 11. Sale of state. things

11.1. Sale of military equipment, the offender receives imprisonment for a period of 5 years and a fine of 25,000 rubles

11.2. Illegal trafficking of weapons, ammunition, as well as theft of weapons or ammunition is punishable by 5 years in prison

## Constitution NChG
Preamble

We, the people of the Chernobyl exclusion zone, united by a common fate and history, proclaim the creation of an Independent Chernobyl State - in the name of freedom, security and preservation of our land.

Our motto: “Independence is our salvation.”

Chapter I. Fundamentals of the system

Article 1. NChG is a sovereign military state.
Article 2. The state religion and ideology is the survival and defense of the territory.
Article 3. The territory of NChG is inviolable. Any violation of borders is equivalent to military aggression.
Article 4. Symbols NChG:

Flag (Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant + radiation, short and clear),

Coat of arms (Motto + our main attraction "Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant"),

Chapter II. Citizens

Article 1. A citizen of NChG is anyone who has officially renounced the citizenship of other countries and has lived in the zone for at least 1 year.
Article 2. Fundamental rights of a citizen:

Right to life

Right to defense

Right to work

The right to participate in politics.
Article 3. Basic duties of a citizen:

Service in the army or government agencies,

Compliance with the Constitution and laws,

Protection of the territory NChG from external and internal threats. 

Chapter III. President and power

Article 1. President NChG is the head of state and the armed forces.
Article 2. The President is elected for 5 years, but can be removed early by the People's Council.
Article 3. The President is obliged:

Lead the army

Sign laws

Appoint ministers

Monitor the security of the state. 

Chapter IV. Authorities

Article 1. The People's Council is a body representing all organizations (groupings) of the zone.
Article 2. The Prosecutor's Office and the Courts NChG are the bodies of justice.
Article 3. The Chernobyl Security Service (ChSO) is an elite body that ensures the security of the president and the state. (Works like in reality "FSO") 

Chapter V. Economy

Article 1. The economy of NChG is based on:

Manufacturing (factories, factories),

Trade (shops, stalls, pharmacies),

Article 2. Currency NChG - Chernobyl Ruble (CR).
Article 3. The state has the right to set control prices for essential goods. 

Chapter VI. Security

Article 1. The armed forces NChG are the guarantor of independence.
Article 2. Every citizen is obliged to undergo military training.
Article 3. The state has the right to apply the death penalty to traitors, traitors and spies. 

Chapter VII. Final provisions

Article 1. Changes to the Constitution can only be made by the People's Council or the President.
Article 2. The Constitution comes into force immediately after its adoption. 

Chapter VIII: Jurisprudence

Article 1. General provisions

Jurisprudence NChG is a system of bodies and officials ensuring justice, protection of rights, compliance with laws and maintaining internal order.,

All persons holding legal positions act in the interests of NChG, subject to the Constitution and general laws.,

Article 2. Main positions in the field of jurisprudence

Prosecutor - supervision over the implementation of laws, initiation of cases.


Investigator - conducting investigations, collecting evidence, identifying the perpetrators.

Judge - hearing cases, making decisions.,

Lawyer - defense of the accused, representation of the interests of citizens.,

Legal consultant - advice on legal issues, assistance in concluding contracts.,

Notary (or equivalent) - certification of transactions, certification of documents.,

Article 3. Responsibilities

The prosecutor is obliged:

Ensure that laws NChG are followed by all citizens and organizations.

Support the charge in court.

Supervise investigative agencies.

The investigator is obliged:

Conduct interrogations, examinations, searches and investigative actions.

Search and collect evidence.

Ensure the objectivity of the investigation.

The judge is obliged:

Maintain independence when making decisions.

Consider cases impartially and only according to the law.

Ensure openness of the process (except for secret matters).

The lawyer is obliged:

Protect the client by all legal means.

Maintain attorney-client privilege.

Do not interfere with the investigation, but defend the rights of the accused.

A legal consultant is obliged to:

Assist citizens and organizations in legal matters.

Warn about possible violations of the law.

The notary is obliged:

Certify transactions and documents.

Check the legality of the parties' actions.
