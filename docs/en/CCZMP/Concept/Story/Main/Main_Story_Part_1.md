## Plot concept for the project Stalker SOC x Chernobyl Criminal Zone MultiPlayer (CCZMP)
Concept: The plot of the global modification for Stalker PM
Story type: Main story (Company) for offline mode
Part 1: Introduction (prologue)
Plot: 
Date: 2023, the war between the Russian Federation is in full swing
The authorities of the Russian Federation learn that Ukraine is developing chemical/bio. weapons in the Chernobyl exclusion zone
They send their intelligence there, which learns about the so-called “PSI radiation”, after which work begins on protection against it. The Russian authorities personally allocate 1.5 billion rubles for the project
The project acquires the status "Secret" 
After completing work on protection against PSI radiation, military columns immediately depart for Pripyat, the operation was called “Chernobyl in 3 days”
Next, they go to the radar, where the Monolith fighters were already waiting for them and turned on the PSI radiation antenna; their defense turned out to be weak against the so-called “Brain Burner”
They began to retreat back, but soldiers of the Ukrainian armed forces were already waiting for them there, and the stalkers also united against them
