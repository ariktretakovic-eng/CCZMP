# STALKER X CCZMP - southwestern area Dead City

The first stage of the criminal segment: ten permanent residents, two yard companies, a brigade intermediary, a buyer and a shop. The area occupies the southwestern courtyards and garages, on the opposite outskirts of the IC base. All residents' starting points are located more than 250 meters from the nearest base employees.

## How to find and start

Come to Dead City and move towards the southwest courtyards. The senior companies, the middleman, the buyer and the shopkeeper are marked on the map. Approach the character on foot closer than 4.5 meters and talk through **F**. During combat, injury or detention, services are not available.

Section **F6 → Southwestern region** contains local relations, available interlocutors, a magazine and appeals to security forces. In F7 debugging there is an item to visit the area; it only works in Dead City and with debugging enabled. A new game is not required for residents to appear: they are created when visiting a location.

The menu uses an existing terminal. **Esc** returns to the previous page; from the main menu closes the window. The close button also works.

## Who is responsible for what

| Interlocutor | Role |
|---|---|
| Gray-haired man and two comrades | Yard company "Garazhnye", connections with the organization "Chetkie" |
| Fang and two comrades | Company "South", a separate local attitude towards the player |
| Decided | Brigade mediator, regular and illegal deliveries, the issue of debt |
| Shilo | Buyer: the real transfer of things for cash |
| Shopkeeper | Limited Supplies and a Call for Help |
| Mechanic | Debtor of the mediator, a separate story with negotiations |

The shop and buyer are located in the inner part of the courtyard next to the armed companies. Personal connections have been established between residents; the general relationships of organizations are not rewritten.

Call signs on the menu indicate the local role. Real full name characters, documents and identity in the registry are preserved. Local relationships with companies and residents are considered separate from the overall reputation of organizations.

## Deliveries and trunk

| Agreement | Recipient | Collateral | Reward | Condition |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Shop supplies | Shopkeeper | 300 rub. | 900 rub. | Available immediately |
| Unregistered Electronics | Shilo | 700 rub. | 2,200 rub. | First successfully deliver supplies |

After accepting the agreement, a **sealed cargo weighing 3.5 kg** is issued in the backpack. The deposit is debited from the player, the reward is reserved from the intermediary’s money. One contract is available at a time. Duration - **45 minutes of active play**; pause and loading do not consume it. Time continues to pass when the player is busy with other things or is in another location.

The cargo can be transported by car and stored in a regular trunk. Before returning it, you need to return it to your backpack and approach the recipient. It is the original copy that is checked: an item with the same name created through F7 will not work. Upon successful delivery, the deposit and reward are returned.

You can bring the original cargo to the intermediary: the deposit will be returned, there will be no reward. Refusal and delay forfeit the collateral and reduce trust. Lost cargo will not be replaced. The contracts of this first stage are final, without repeated receipt of awards.

Illegal cargo can be physically transferred to employee MVD or IC through the appeals section. The contract is considered broken and the deposit is withheld. The transfer is recorded separately from the application and is not an automatic sentence.

## Buying and shop

Shiloh buys tradable items from the backpack for **35% of the base cost based on condition**. For items for which the existing gaming system has already recorded a theft mark, the coefficient is **25%**. The mere presence of someone else's name on the device is not considered proven theft.

Items in slots, active equipment, quest items, prohibited items for trading, and sealed cargo are not accepted. The buyer receives the original item, and the player receives real cash from his wallet. The message history, settings and identity of the owner of the sold device are saved.

Shila’s initial budget is 30,000 rubles. It is not restored at loading. Six bandages, four breads, three bottles of vodka and two batteries are added to the shop once; Suitable supplies from the NPC's original inventory may also be sold. The real supply is being used up. Rebooting does not update the assortment.

## Help the shopkeeper

1. Take a request from the shopkeeper.
2. Talk to Sedym.
3. If you trust “Garage” from 5 or belong to a government organization, you can negotiate without payment. A normal successful delivery gives the required 5 points.
4. Another option is to pay **800 rubles with your own money**.
5. Return to the shopkeeper for the promised **600 rubles**. The payment is one-time and comes from his wallet.

## Mechanic's duty

The mechanic has a debt of **1,500 rubles**. After his request, you can:

- pay the entire debt to the intermediary from your cash;
- after a successful regular delivery and if you trust the team from 5, ask to write off 500 rubles, then inform the mechanic. He will transfer the remaining thousand to the intermediary from his own wallet.

The written off portion is a change in liability, not a creation of money. All calculations and solutions are saved.

## Demanding money in the yard

If the player approaches the elder and at least two members of the company are nearby, a conversation is possible with the demand for **500 rubles**. It is not included for government employees and with local trust from 15. Replay is limited to **30 minutes of active play** for each company.

You can pay, agree with trust from 5, refuse and leave, or openly start a conflict. A simple refusal reduces the relationship, but does not in itself start a fight. A direct challenge makes the company hostile. If a player attacks a villager, members of his company who saw the attack may also join the fight. Hostile characters cannot sell items or submit contracts through the menu.

## Calling MVD and IC

Come closer than 4.5 meters to a live, accessible employee of MVD or IC. Open **F6 → Southwestern District → Appeals to MVD / IC**, select the recorded demand for money. A repeated application for the same event is not created.

The neighborhood journal stores the event experienced. Only upon request, a statement record is created in the existing incident system. The applicant's words alone do not create a warrant, conviction, or omniscient search. The transferred illegal cargo is in the employee's real inventory and has a separate record of the transfer; its significance for the case requires verification.

## Conservation and neighborhood life

Residents are created once for the current save branch. Dead and deleted ones are not replaced by copies. Cash, store supplies, relationships, debt, journal, statements, original cargo, collateral and contract stages are saved with the game. loading of an earlier save returns the state of that moment.

During the day, residents move along short local routes, and at night they stand at their points longer. Combat, injury, conversation and detention have priority over the everyday route. The physical life of NPCs is subject to the normal radius of Anomaly's online simulation.

The first stage does not include a rework of the entire economy, OCG wars for areas, the capture of enterprises, full-fledged player imprisonment, or a separate system of terrorist operations. For subsequent stories, there are already permanent participants, objects, relationships and records of events.
